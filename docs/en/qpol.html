QPoL · Research · Nothing is published

What if the resource protecting a consensus were neither energy nor capital?

QPoL is a research effort, not a piece of software. This page states which problem it attacks and where it stands. It does not state how it goes about it — and that page will not exist until the intellectual-property question is settled.

▲

No published code, no network, no token, no dates. There is nothing to download, nothing to join and nothing to buy.

The problem

Every decentralised guarantee is bought with a scarce resource

A network without a central authority has to stop one person from posing as thousands. The defence is always the same: tie the right to participate to a resource that cannot be manufactured at will. The choice of that resource then determines everything else — and in particular who ends up controlling the network.

MechanismScarce resourceWhere concentration leads
Proof of work Energy and computation Towards regions with cheap electricity
Proof of stake Capital Towards capital holders, and a handful of hosting providers

In both cases the resource is relocatable. Nothing prevents gathering it in a single place, and that is exactly what happens. One operator can simulate thousands of distinct participants from a single machine room, provided they pay.

The ambition

Looking for a resource that cannot be gathered

QPoL explores another route: a resource whose scarcity comes not from a purchase price but from a physical constraint — something an operator cannot concentrate in one place, by construction, whatever their budget.

That is all this page will say about the method. The mechanism, its guarantees and its limitations are the subject of written work that is not public.

Why this page stops here

Publicly describing a mechanism before filing a patent application can destroy its patentability — in Europe, novelty is absolute and there is no grace period. Until that question is settled with a patent attorney, the detail stays unpublished. This is not mystification: it is the direct consequence of a schedule.

Where the work stands

What has been done, in non-technical terms

  • A design document setting out the mechanism and its assumptions.
  • A theoretical result, written and checked, bounding what the mechanism guarantees.
  • A viability study testing the idea against real hardware constraints.
  • A simulator comparing the resistance obtained with that of an existing approach.
  • A prior-art review and a freedom-to-operate pre-analysis, currently under review by an attorney.
What it is not

It is not a product, not a chain, not a fundraise. No timeline is announced, and this work may well remain a theoretical contribution. The honesty applied to Obscura applies here too: a research result is not a running system.
